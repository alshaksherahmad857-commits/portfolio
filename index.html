Who is Ahmad?

In short — a Flutter developer who cares about building things that actually matter, not just interfaces that look good.

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Applied Science Private University (ASU) in Jordan, graduated with a Good standing. My focus is mobile app development with Flutter, and I took my graduation project seriously — as Team Lead on Able+, a platform that helps people in Jordan find accessible services, charities, tutors, and community support for people with disabilities.

Beyond Able+, I've delivered full-stack websites and mobile apps for real use cases: a medical devices and supplies platform, a travel company's website, and a travel company's mobile app. I work with Clean Architecture and prefer organized, maintainable code over quick patch-jobs.
